# Thank you to the authors

Thank you to Jiajie Zhao, Jianxing Wang, Junjie Yang, Zhiwei Bai, and Yaoyu Zhang for the theoretical development of gradient-flow dynamics and implicit bias in diagonal linear networks, the explicit Algorithm 1 appendix fixture, and the detailed discussion of Structural Invariant Manifolds.

This repository is an independent, scoped audit. It preserves the paper’s source identity, cites the original work, records the paper’s claim-production path, and clearly separates the bounded exact-arithmetic toy from theorem-level results that remain unverified locally.
